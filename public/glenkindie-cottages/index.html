Tucked along a quiet stretch of Upper Donside, Glenkindie Cottages offer a simple kind of luxury: clean air, wide skies, and the ease that comes with being surrounded by nature. Whether you’re visiting for a long weekend or settling in for a season, these thoughtfully restored cottages provide a comfortable home base for exploring the area—or simply staying still.

Originally built to house estate workers, the cottages have been carefully updated to blend historic character with modern convenience. Inside, you’ll find warm, welcoming spaces with wood-burning stoves, well-equipped kitchens, and generous living areas. Each cottage is distinct, but all are furnished with attention to detail and a sense of quiet charm. Views from the windows stretch out across farmland, woodlands, and distant hills—changing with the seasons but always steady.

Glenkindie House and its gardens are just a short walk from the cottages, as are several walking paths that follow the contours of the Don valley. It’s not uncommon to see red squirrels darting through the trees, or buzzards circling above open fields. And when night falls, the skies here darken properly—ideal for stargazing on clear evenings.

Six cottages in total

Chapelton, Dalgrassick, Greenstyle, Home Farm, The Brow and Tornhatnach. Each offers different sleeping arrangements, suitable for couples, families, or small groups. All are self-catering and dog-friendly by arrangement. Guests are provided with fresh linens, Wi-Fi, and access to laundry facilities. A welcome basket greets arrivals, typically stocked with essentials and a few local touches. For those staying longer, the cottages are well set up for day-to-day living.

Booking

Booking is straightforward through the Glenkindie Cottages website, where you can check availability, compare cottages, and secure your stay. If you have specific questions or accessibility needs, the estate team is easy to reach and happy to help.

While the cottages feel removed from the rush of daily life, they’re not far from anything you might need. The villages of Alford and Strathdon are close by for groceries, cafés, and small shops. Castles, trailheads, museums, and viewpoints are all within a short drive, and the estate itself offers plenty of space to walk, read, and breathe deeply.

Memorable

These cottages weren’t designed as destinations—but over time, they’ve become just that. Places where families return year after year. Places where time feels a little slower and more spacious. And places where a simple stay becomes something quietly memorable.
